# Getting Started

This course is application only. The general steps in [Joining a Cohort]({{ '/courses/zoomcamp-logistics/joining/' | relative_url }}) describe the open zoomcamps, so use this page for the steps that apply here.

## Apply

1. [Apply here](https://courses.datatalks.club/register/production-ai-agents-generative-media/) and tell us what you are building.
2. We review every application, usually within 2 business days.
3. Approved applicants join the live cohort: shared deadlines, peer-reviewed homework, and the capstone.

The cohort starts on Monday, November 16, 2026, and applications are open until Monday, November 30, 2026. Module 1 runs two weeks, so you can still apply after the start and catch up.

If you are not approved, all materials are public, and you can follow along on your own.

## Get GPU credits

Approved applicants get GPU credits through the [Nebius AI Builder Program](https://dev.nebius.com/builders), which is free to join. Usage beyond the credits is billed to your account. If you follow along without applying, you pay for your own GPU usage.

## Use the course platform

The [course platform](https://courses.datatalks.club/production-ai-agents-generative-media/) shows the schedule and deadlines. Homework and capstone submissions are done there after approval.

## Ask questions

Join the [DataTalks.Club Slack workspace](https://datatalks.club/slack.html) and bring your questions to the weekly live office hour. Before asking, read [Asking Questions]({{ '/courses/zoomcamp-logistics/asking-questions/' | relative_url }}).

## Course workflow

1. Watch the pre-recorded lectures for each module and read the module README.
2. Do the homework in Modules 1 to 3.
3. Write the capstone proposal in Module 1 and build the capstone after Module 4.
4. Complete the peer reviews assigned on the course platform.

Live sessions in the cohort are the pre-course Live Q&A, the course launch, and the weekly office hour. Recordings are collected in a YouTube playlist linked from the repository.

See the [curriculum]({{ '/courses/production-ai-agents-generative-media/curriculum/' | relative_url }}) for the full schedule.
