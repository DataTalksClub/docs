# FAQ

A short list of the most common questions. The full course FAQ is on [datatalks.club/faq](https://datatalks.club/faq/).

## Who is this course for?

Working builders running open or custom GPU media models: AI and ML engineers adding image, video, or audio generation to real systems, MLOps and ML platform engineers who own the batch pipeline and the cost line, and technical founders who need to know when self-hosting beats per-call media APIs.

## What do I need to know before I start?

Python, Docker basics, HTTP APIs, and the command line. Prior LLM or MLOps course experience helps but is not required.

## Do I need my own GPU?

No. The models run as Jobs and Endpoints on Nebius Serverless AI, so there is no GPU or cluster to manage. Applicants get GPU credits through the [Nebius AI Builder Program](https://dev.nebius.com/builders). Usage beyond the credits is billed to your account. If you follow along without applying, you need your own GPU access.

## Can I apply after the course starts?

Yes, until Monday, November 30, 2026. The course starts on Monday, November 16, and Module 1 runs two weeks, so you can catch up. [Apply here](https://courses.datatalks.club/register/production-ai-agents-generative-media/).

## Do I have to attend live sessions?

No. Lectures are pre-recorded, and live classes are not mandatory. The pre-course Live Q&A, the course launch, and the weekly office hour are live.

## Why do I have to apply?

The live cohort has limited seats, so it is application only. Tell us what you are building. We review every application, usually within 2 business days.

## What if I am not approved?

All materials are public (MIT) in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/open-source-llm-zoomcamp), so you can follow along on your own with your own GPU access.

## Is the course free?

Yes. The course is free and open source. The GPU workloads cost money: applicants get credits through the Nebius AI Builder Program, and usage beyond the credits is billed. Each module reports cost per asset, so you know what a run costs before you scale it.

## Am I locked into Nebius?

No. The code is OpenAI- and S3-compatible, so you can run it elsewhere. Nebius Serverless AI and Nebius Token Factory are the reference platform for the code-alongs and homework.

## Is there a certificate?

Not confirmed yet. We will update the course pages when that is decided.

## Can I use the generated media commercially?

Open-weights models come with their own licences. Check each model's licence before commercial use.

## Do I need special hardware, an OS or Docker locally?

No minimum. CPU-only work and the agent run on a laptop (Python 3.11 is enough), and GPU work runs on Nebius Serverless AI. Docker is used to build the container for Module 1, so install it before then. Nebius also offers a hosted Jupyter Lab environment as a cloud alternative for older laptops.

## Is late homework accepted?

Not after the submission form closes. Catch up at your own pace and aim for the second capstone attempt.

## What makes a capstone stand out?

Explain the challenges you hit and how you solved them (cost, errors, performance), and show that you understand the system rather than only prompting a coding agent.

## I applied and still see Pending. What should I do?

Applications are reviewed in batches, usually within 2 business days. No further step is needed.

## Where can I ask more questions?

Check the [full FAQ](https://datatalks.club/faq/), ask in the [DataTalks.Club Slack](https://datatalks.club/slack.html), and bring questions to the weekly live office hour. See [Asking Questions]({{ '/courses/zoomcamp-logistics/asking-questions/' | relative_url }}) first.
