# DataTalks.Club Slack: Channels and How to Get Help

The DataTalks.Club Slack is where our community talks. Thousands of data scientists, ML engineers, data engineers, and AI practitioners use it to ask questions, discuss their work, get career advice, follow our courses, and talk with book authors.

## How to Join

Request access on the [Slack page](/slack). Tell us a little about yourself, or ask an existing member you know to vouch for you. Course participants are approved automatically. Once you are approved, you get a single-use invitation to join the workspace. Then join the channels you are interested in.

If something goes wrong, use the "Need help with Slack?" option on the same page and the community team will help.

## How to Get Help

Ask in a public channel, not in a DM. That way others can help you, and the answer helps the next person with the same question. A few habits get you a faster answer:

- Pick the one channel that fits your question and post it there once. Course questions go to your course channel, career questions to `#career-questions`.
- Check the FAQ first. Every zoomcamp has a [FAQ](/faq/), and Slack search often finds the same error already answered.
- Don't ask to ask. Post the question itself, not "Can I ask about X?".
- Put the details in a thread. Write a short summary in the channel and paste the error, code, and logs in the thread.
- Copy and paste errors and code as text inside triple backticks. Don't post screenshots of code, and never photos of your screen.
- Don't tag instructors. They see questions in the course channel, and a tag makes other participants less likely to answer.
- Don't delete a question once it is answered. Reply with what fixed it instead.

The full rules live in the [Community Guidelines]({{ '/general/guidelines/' | relative_url }}): [Code of Conduct]({{ '/general/guidelines/code-of-conduct/' | relative_url }}), [Discussions]({{ '/general/guidelines/discussions/' | relative_url }}), [Asking for Help]({{ '/general/guidelines/asking-for-help/' | relative_url }}), and [Promotion]({{ '/general/guidelines/promotion/' | relative_url }}).
